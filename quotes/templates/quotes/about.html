Who is Spider-Man

The "friendly neighborhood" Spider-Man is a fictional superhero that resides in Queens, New York City. His alias is "Peter Parker", who was a high school student bitten by a radioactive spider. Throughout the films' franchise, there are 3 actors who have famously played Spider-Man. They are Tobey Maguire, Andrew Garfield, and Tom Holland; all of which are shown in this website, in supersuit or without.
